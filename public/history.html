Our story

A house of worship, built by a community.

Islamic Center of Greater Phoenix, also known as Masjid Bilal, is a place for daily prayer, Friday Jummah, and shared community life.

From then to now

A former church transformed into a masjid.

With the help of the community, this property was transformed into a beautiful masjid. Today, the prayer hall welcomes worshippers for all five daily prayers and Friday Jummah.

The work continues through the generosity of people who believe in building a lasting place of worship in South Phoenix.
